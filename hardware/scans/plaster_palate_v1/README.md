# Plaster Palate Master v1

First plaster master of user's palate, captured June 2026.

## Provenance
- **Impression material:** Alginate (Lowe's, $8)
- **Tray:** Stock impression tray
- **Cast material:** Plaster of Paris
- **Date impressed:** Late May 2026
- **Date cast:** Within 24 hours of impression (alginate drifts after 24h)

## Capture status
- [ ] Raw scan captured
- [ ] Mesh cleaned in Blender
- [ ] Exported as `clean.stl` for CAD reference

## Subdirectories
- `raw/` — original scan output and source photos (LFS-tracked)
- `clean.stl` — final cleaned mesh, ready as Blender import (LFS-tracked, generated)

## Notes
Plaster is white and texture-poor, which is bad for photogrammetry. Before scanning:
- Apply light dusting of baby powder or sprinkle of fine pepper for visual features, OR
- Use a structured-light / LiDAR scanner that doesn't depend on visual texture
